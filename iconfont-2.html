Lucide began as a fork of Feather — a minimal icon set, drawn on a 24-pixel grid with consistent stroke weight and a single, quiet personality. Where its predecessor favored restraint, Lucide has grown into a larger family while holding onto the same geometric discipline.

Each icon is an inline SVG. No font file to serve, no glyph substitution to debug. What you see in the source is what renders on screen. The trade-off is weight — but for an editorial page like this, that transparency is the point.

We chose a small set here, each picked for a specific mood rather than utility. A flame is not a "fire icon" — it is a gesture, a breath, a pulse. A gem is not a "diamond" — it is weight, clarity, something held up to the light. The curation is the work.

Rendered at 1.25px stroke, the Lucide family feels drawn rather than stamped. Compare that to the previous page's Font Awesome — the difference in voice is audible, even when both are silent.
